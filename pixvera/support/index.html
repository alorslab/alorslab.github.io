Frequently Asked Questions

Which features are free?

Compress, Resize, Convert, Remove Metadata, PDF Maker, Document Scanner, OCR and text recognition, and the creation of standard and text-based PDFs are available without an artificial Pixvera Pro limit.

You can process multiple photos within the technical capabilities of Pixvera and your device.

What is included with Smart Cleanup Pro?

Pixvera Pro unlocks Smart Cleanup for Screenshots, Large Photos, Duplicates, and Similar Photos. It also lets you optimize photos selected from Smart Cleanup results.

Duplicate and similar-photo results are automated recommendations, so review every selection before taking action.

Which Pixvera Pro plans are available?

Pixvera Pro is available as Monthly for $2.99 per month, Annual for $20.99 per year, or Lifetime for a $40.99 one-time purchase.

The App Store displays the final price, currency, and applicable taxes for your country or region before purchase.

How does the 3-day free trial work?

Eligible Monthly or Annual subscriptions may include a 3-day free trial. The trial appears only when Apple determines that your Apple Account is eligible for the introductory offer.

When the trial ends, the selected subscription becomes paid and renews automatically unless you cancel it before the trial ends.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Pixvera

You can also manage subscriptions through the App Store. Cancel before the trial or current billing period ends to prevent the next charge. Deleting Pixvera does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Pixvera → Settings → Restore Purchases

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original subscription or Lifetime purchase. Restoration depends on the purchase status provided by Apple.

Does Lifetime remain available after reinstalling Pixvera?

Yes. Lifetime is linked to the Apple Account used for the purchase and can be restored after reinstalling Pixvera when you use the same account.

Lifetime does not include a trial. Access may end if Apple refunds, revokes, or invalidates the transaction.

Why is the free trial not showing?

Apple controls introductory-offer eligibility. A user can receive only one introductory offer within the applicable subscription group.

If your Apple Account previously used an introductory offer for a subscription in that group, the 3-day trial may not be available.

How does Smart Cleanup handle iCloud-only photos?

Smart Cleanup analyzes photos whose originals are available locally on your device. It does not initiate downloads of originals stored only in iCloud.

An iCloud-only item may not appear in Smart Cleanup results until its original is available locally.

Does Pixvera delete original photos automatically?

No. Pixvera does not automatically delete original photos.

A photo is replaced or deleted only after you choose the relevant action and confirm it. Always review Smart Cleanup selections before confirming a change.

Does Pixvera upload my photos, scans, or OCR text?

No. Pixvera processes photos, scans, recognized text, and created PDFs locally on your device and does not send that content to Alors Lab servers.
