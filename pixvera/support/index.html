Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photos can I process for free?

The free version of Pixvera supports up to 3 photos per batch.

Pixvera Pro unlocks unlimited batch processing within the application's technical limits.

Does Pixvera upload my photos?

No.

Pixvera is designed to process photos directly on your device.

What image formats are supported?

Pixvera supports JPEG, HEIC, and PNG for its main photo tools.

Will Pixvera delete my original photos?

No.

Pixvera creates new processed files and does not automatically delete your original photos.

How do I restore Pixvera Pro?

Pixvera → Settings → Restore Purchases

Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Why did my compressed photo become larger?

Some images are already highly compressed.

Depending on the original format and selected quality settings, recompression can occasionally produce a similar or larger file.

Can I remove GPS metadata?

Yes.

Remove Metadata → Location

Or use Make Photo Private to remove multiple privacy-related metadata categories.
