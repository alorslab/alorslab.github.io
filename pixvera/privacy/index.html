Pixvera Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Pixvera processes your photos, documents, metadata, OCR text, and PDFs on your device. No account is required.

1. Introduction

Pixvera is a photo and document utility application developed by Alors Lab. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Pixvera.

Pixvera is designed around local processing. It does not require you to create an account.

2. Local Processing

Pixvera processes photos and documents locally on your device. This includes compression, resizing, format conversion, metadata removal, document scanning, OCR text recognition, PDF creation, and Smart Cleanup analysis.

Your photos, photo metadata, recognized OCR text, and created PDFs are not uploaded or sent to Alors Lab servers.

3. Photo Library Access

Pixvera uses the Photo Library permission to let you select and analyze photos and, when you choose the relevant feature, save, replace, or delete photos.

Pixvera does not replace or delete a photo automatically. Replacement or deletion occurs only after you take the corresponding action and confirm it.

4. Camera and Document Scanning

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a document. Captured pages are processed for document scanning and PDF creation on your device.

5. Smart Cleanup and Local Photo Analysis

Smart Cleanup analyzes photos that are available locally on your device to identify screenshots, large photos, duplicates, and similar photos.

During its library scan, Smart Cleanup does not request network downloads. A photo is included in the scan only when its file is already available on the device.

6. Temporary Files and Local Records

Pixvera may create temporary files for scanning, photo processing, OCR, and PDF creation inside the application's local container. These temporary files are removed after processing or when they are no longer needed.

File timestamps may be checked locally to identify and remove outdated temporary data. Pixvera also uses UserDefaults to store application settings and local operational records needed to manage optimized photos and the Pixvera album.

These local records may include Photo Library identifiers, the type and date of an optimization, image dimensions, file-size values, and the selected optimization preset. They do not contain the photo or document content itself.

Alors Lab does not treat these local-only operations as collection or transmission of your data. They do not send information to Alors Lab and are not used to track you.

7. Personal Information, Analytics, and Advertising

Pixvera does not require an account and does not collect profile information through the application.

Pixvera does not use third-party advertising, third-party analytics, or user-tracking services. Alors Lab does not sell or transfer personal data through Pixvera.

8. In-App Purchases

Pixvera Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. Alors Lab does not receive or store your payment card information.

Pixvera receives purchase or subscription status from Apple on your device only as needed to determine whether Pixvera Pro features should be available. Apple handles purchase records and payment information under its own terms and privacy policies.

9. Support Communications

If you contact support by email, Alors Lab receives the email address and any information you choose to include in your message. This information is used only to respond to and resolve your support request.

Do not include photos, documents, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request and you choose to share it.

10. Data Retention

Alors Lab does not retain your photos, photo metadata, OCR text, or generated PDFs on its servers because Pixvera does not send that content to Alors Lab.

Temporary processing files are kept locally only as needed for the relevant operation and cleanup. Local settings and operational records remain on your device until they are updated or removed by Pixvera, or deleted with the application. Purchase records are maintained by Apple. Information you voluntarily send to support is used only for handling your request.

11. Children's Privacy

Pixvera is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not require accounts or create user profiles.

If a child or their parent or guardian contacts support, only the information voluntarily included in that communication is received and used to respond.

12. Security

Pixvera reduces exposure of your content by performing its core photo and document operations locally on your device. Access to photos and the camera remains subject to iOS permissions and device security.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for securing your device and maintaining backups of important photos and documents.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to Pixvera's features, data practices, or applicable requirements.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
