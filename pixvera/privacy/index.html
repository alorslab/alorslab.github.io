Pixvera Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Photos are processed locally. Pixvera does not require an account or upload your photos to Alors Lab servers.

1. Introduction

Pixvera is a photo utility application developed by Alors Lab.

Pixvera provides tools to compress, resize, convert, and remove metadata from photos.

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixvera handles information when you use the application.

2. Photos and Image Data

Pixvera processes selected photos locally on your device.

Pixvera does not upload, transmit, or store your photos on Alors Lab servers.

Photo processing, including compression, resizing, format conversion, and metadata removal, is performed on the device.

3. Personal Information

Pixvera does not require you to create an account.

Pixvera does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other personally identifiable information through the application.

4. Photo Metadata

Photos may contain metadata such as:

  • GPS location
  • device information
  • camera information
  • date and time information
  • copyright or author information

Pixvera can display and remove selected metadata locally on your device.

Pixvera does not transmit this metadata to Alors Lab.

5. In-App Purchases

Pixvera may offer Pixvera Pro as a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Alors Lab does not receive or store your payment card information.

Apple may process information related to purchases according to Apple's own privacy policies.

6. Analytics and Advertising

Pixvera does not currently use third-party advertising networks.

Pixvera does not currently use third-party analytics services.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

7. Data Retention

Because Pixvera does not upload your photos or operate user accounts, Alors Lab does not retain your photos or image metadata on external servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Pixvera is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because Pixvera does not require accounts and does not collect personal information through the application, no user profile information is stored by Alors Lab.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in Pixvera's features or data practices.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact:
